Who we are

Sahayog is an AI-based support platform built for entrepreneurs from marginalized backgrounds — women, rural communities, persons with disabilities, low-income families, and minority and tribal groups. We help you discover government and private schemes you actually qualify for, in your own language.

How we help

We listen to what you do — by voice or by typing — and use AI to match you with the right schemes. Then we guide you step by step through the application, in plain language, so you're never left to figure it out alone.

The barrier isn't ability. It's access.

Many capable entrepreneurs never reach the support they qualify for — not because they aren't ready, but because the system wasn't built for them.

How can I fund my business?

A plain overview of where support could come from — not a promise of any of it.

Government schemes

Subsidies, grants and low-interest loans matched to your profile.

Formal financing

General bank loans and institutional lending — eligibility set by the lender.

Entrepreneurship programs

Training, incubation and mentorship support alongside any funding.

Self-funding

Starting small with personal savings, at a general educational level.

Other support

Anything else in the verified scheme dataset that applies to you.

Funding availability and eligibility depend on the relevant institution's own criteria and approval process — Sahayog helps you discover options, it does not guarantee funding.
